Everything you need to know about working with DigiMarkly.

What services does DigiMarkly offer?

expand_more

We specialize in three core areas: Website Design & Development (from landing pages to portfolio and e-commerce sites), Meta Ads Management on Facebook and Instagram, and Local & On-page SEO to help nearby customers discover you on Google.

Do you build websites for small businesses?

expand_more

Yes! In fact, small businesses, cafés, local clinics, retail shops, independent consultants, and service providers are our core focus. We build sites that look exceptional, load quickly on mobile, and are easy to maintain.

How much does a website cost?

expand_more

Our websites start from ₹7,000 for standard professional landing pages and small business sites. We give you a clear, itemized proposal before starting so there are zero surprise charges.

Do you manage Facebook and Instagram Ads?

expand_more

Yes, we handle the entire ad lifecycle: graphic creatives, compelling ad copy, precise local audience targeting, campaign setup in Meta Business Suite, and weekly performance tracking.

Do you provide SEO services?

expand_more

Yes, we handle Google Business Profile setup, local keyword research, and on-page optimization so nearby customers looking for your services in Pune or your local city find you first.

How do I get started?

expand_more

Just send us a quick WhatsApp message or fill out our contact form below. We’ll discuss your business goals, see if we’re a good match, and give you an actionable recommendation with zero sales pressure.

Can you work with businesses in Pune?

expand_more

Absolutely! We are based in Pune and love meeting local business owners in person over coffee, as well as collaborating remotely with businesses across India.
